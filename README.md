🚀 What is CineTime?

CineTime is a powerful, all-in-one entertainment app for Android that brings together:

· 🎥 Thousands of Movies — Browse, search, and stream popular films
· 📺 Endless TV Series — Full seasons and episodes at your fingertips
· 📡 Live TV Channels — Watch free-to-air channels from around the world
· 🔍 Smart Search — Find any title instantly
· 🎭 Browse by Genre, Year, Rating & Actors — Discover content your way
· 📥 Download Support — Save movies and episodes via 1DM browser

Built with a sleek, modern interface and powered by TMDB + IPTV-org APIs, CineTime delivers a premium streaming experience — completely free.

📥 How to Download Movies & Episodes

CineTime uses the powerful 1DM browser to handle downloads. Here's how:

Step 1: Install 1DM

Get it free from Google Play:
⬇️ Download 1DM

Step 2: Find Your Content

Browse to any movie or episode in CineTime.

Step 3: Open in 1DM

· Tap the ⚙️ Options button on the player
· Select "Open in 1DM"
· The download page opens inside the 1DM browser

Step 4: Wait for Detection

· Do not close the page
· Let the video play for 10-30 seconds
· 1DM will detect the stream automatically

Step 5: Download

· Tap the floating download button in 1DM
· Choose your quality and start downloading!

💡 Tip: Use the "Copy URL" option if 1DM doesn't auto-detect.

🛠️ Technical Details

Powered By

· TMDB — Movie & TV metadata

Architecture

```
CineTime/
├── pub-index.html      # Main app (home, search, tabs)
├── player.html         # Video player (movies, series, TV)
├── download.html       # 1DM download helper
├── genres.html         # Genre browser

```

· Full-screen video support

· Encrypted assets for security

❓ FAQ

Why doesn't a movie appear?

CineTime only shows content that's available on the streaming backend. If a title is missing, it's not currently streamable.

The video won't play — what do I do?

1. Check your internet connection
2. Try a different server/quality
3. Clear the app cache
4. Restart the app

How do I download videos?

Use the 1DM browser integration — see the Download Guide above.

Is CineTime free?

Yes! CineTime is completely free with no subscriptions or hidden fees.

Will there be ads?

Some content may include ads from third-party streaming sources. CineTime itself doesn't inject ads.
